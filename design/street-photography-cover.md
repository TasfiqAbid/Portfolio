## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: square cover artwork for a street photography portfolio project, designed to sit in a collage of graphic design posters on a black website. Primary request: create a sophisticated editorial concept cover for a project titled Street Photography. This is clearly a designed conceptual cover, not a purported actual photograph from the artist's real project. Scene: a timeless layered South Asian urban street, narrow old buildings, a cycle rickshaw and a few anonymous walking silhouettes, strong long diagonal afternoon shadows and shafts of warm light. Visual treatment: art-directed photographic collage / analog contact-sheet-inspired composition with crisp architectural lines, deep charcoal and warm off-white, restrained amber light accents. Keep main street composition in the middle and lower half. Upper-left quarter should have quiet nearly-black architectural shadow, suitable for a cream title which will be added in website code. No lettering, no text, no numbers, no logos, no watermarks, no borders or external mockup, no cookie branding, no artificial blur or heavy grain. Straight-on flat full-bleed square artwork, premium restrained photography book cover feeling, high-resolution sharp detail. Color palette: charcoal black, warm ivory, muted amber; warm street atmosphere with strong photographic depth. Do not create identifiable real people.
